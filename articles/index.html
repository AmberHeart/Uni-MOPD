Are merge coefficients worth tuning before distillation?

The training recipe

What does the choice of distillation loss change?

Reverse KL, forward KL and ToDi differ by domain, and a distribution beats a sampled token.

How should MOPD sample queries across domains?

Equal query shares are not equal supervision: STEM carries most of the tokens.

Where does the Open-MOPD recipe lose ground, and what does the framework cost?

The Open-MOPD recipe stalls on STEM, and the framework runs full-vocabulary distillation at 266 s per update.
